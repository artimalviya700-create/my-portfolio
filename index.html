My Journey

Hi, I'm Arti Malviya! I am currently in my 2nd year of the 5-Year Integrated M.Tech in Information Technology based in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. Before my university journey, I completed my schooling at Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, Chhindwara, which instilled in me deep values of discipline, curiosity, and teamwork.
